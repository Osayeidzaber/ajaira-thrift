SIZE CHART EXAMPLES - copy one into Shopify admin
Content > Metaobjects > Size chart > Add entry
(fill Title, Match words, Rows)

First line of Rows = column names. Then one size per line.
Separate cells with commas. Use (in) or (cm) in the column names
so the in/cm switch works.

TIPS
- Column names the drawing understands:
  Jackets/vests: Chest, Length, Shoulder, Sleeve, Waist, Hem
  Pants: Waist, Hip, Thigh, Rise, Inseam, Length, Leg opening
- One product only? Open the product > Product metafields >
  fill "Size chart title" and "Size chart rows" (same format).
- These numbers are examples. Replace them with your real measurements.
