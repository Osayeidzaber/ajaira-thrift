## Refund & return policy

You can return any item within **14 days** of receiving it.

**How to return**
1. Message us on WhatsApp (+880 1992-420839) or Instagram (@ajairathrift) with your order number and the item you want to return.
2. We'll confirm the return and send you the return address.
3. Pack the item securely and send it back within 7 days of our confirmation.

**Conditions**
- Items must be unworn, unwashed and in the same condition you received them, with any tags still attached.
- Every piece is second-hand and one of a kind; wear described in the product listing is not a reason for return.
- Return shipping costs are paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or was not as described.

**Refunds**
- Once we receive and check the item, we refund the full item price to your original payment method within 7 business days.
- Original shipping charges are not refunded.

**Damaged or wrong item**
Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos, and we'll arrange a free return and full refund.

## Shipping policy

**Processing time:** orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 business days.

**Delivery times (after dispatch)**
- Inside Dhaka: 1–3 business days
- Outside Dhaka: 3–5 business days

**Shipping costs** are shown at checkout before you pay.

**Tracking:** you'll receive a tracking number by SMS or email once your order ships.

**Cash on delivery** may be available for some areas; options are shown at checkout.

If your parcel hasn't arrived within the expected time, message us on WhatsApp (+880 1992-420839) and we'll check with the courier.

## Privacy policy

Ajaira Thrift ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

**What we collect**
- Details you give us when you order: name, phone number, email, delivery address.
- Payment information, which is processed securely by our payment providers. We never see or store your full card details.
- Basic browsing data (such as pages viewed) collected by Shopify to run and improve the store.

**How we use it**
- To process, ship and support your orders.
- To send order updates, and marketing messages only if you've agreed to receive them.
- To prevent fraud and keep the store secure.

**Sharing**
We share your details only with services needed to run the store, such as Shopify, payment providers and delivery couriers. We never sell your personal information.

**Your choices**
You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link in any email.
